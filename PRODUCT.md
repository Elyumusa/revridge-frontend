## Platform

## Users

People learning to invest and plan their finances, including first-time investors. Revridge is based in Zambia; Alpaca determines eligibility for its accounts and services.

## Product Purpose

Revridge connects learning, goals, savings and net-worth tracking with an Invest experience focused on U.S. securities through Alpaca Connect. Non-investing features remain usable without an Alpaca account. Brand promise: **Build wealth beyond borders.** Core journey: **Learn → Invest → Grow**.

## Current Investing Status

- Paper trading with virtual money is available through an authorized Alpaca paper account. Paper balances and orders are separate from real net worth and holdings.
- Live trading through Revridge awaits Alpaca approval and a separate release. Do not describe it as available or imply a date.
- Each investor opens and owns their Alpaca account directly. Alpaca handles account opening, identity checks, funding, withdrawals, statements, execution, settlement, custody, and account administration.
- Revridge requests permission through Alpaca Connect to view account data and, separately, to trade. A read-only grant cannot place or cancel orders. Revridge does not request an Alpaca password, bank credentials, Trading API keys, or identity documents for Alpaca onboarding.
- LuSE shares and unit trusts are absent from the current Invest experience. Do not present historical LuSE order routing as a current feature.
- An accepted or submitted order is not necessarily filled. Market data and account information may be delayed.
- Revridge does not provide personalized investment advice. Investing involves possible loss of capital.

## Other Capabilities

Learning, goals, savings, net worth, and the generic client-side Grow Calculator remain part of the product. Savings goals are tracking tools, not accounts holding money. The calculator provides illustrative estimates, not a current rate or forecast. Android store and iOS beta links exist on this site; verify the released app builds before promising that a specific Connect feature is in those store builds.
